# 📖 Overview

**Kisan Saathi** is an AI-powered decision support platform designed for **Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)** under the **Watershed Development Component (WDC) 2.0**.

The platform automatically measures income growth, identifies which government interventions actually increased farmer income, predicts future performance using AI, and provides actionable recommendations for policymakers.

Unlike traditional reporting systems, Kisan Saathi performs **causal impact analysis**, helping governments understand the effectiveness of individual interventions while separating external factors such as weather and market price fluctuations.

---

# 🚀 Problem Statement

Government agencies currently evaluate FPO performance using manual reports and spreadsheets.

This process is:

- Time-consuming
- Error-prone
- Difficult to scale
- Unable to measure actual intervention impact
- Lacks predictive intelligence

As a result, policymakers cannot accurately determine which interventions deliver the highest returns.

---

# 💡 Solution

Kisan Saathi provides an AI-driven platform that:

- 📈 Measures FPO income growth automatically
- 🤖 Predicts future income using Machine Learning
- 🌦 Removes weather and market bias using Causal AI
- 📊 Generates district and state-level dashboards
- ⚠ Detects high-risk FPOs early
- 🎯 Recommends the best interventions
- 🔍 Performs What-If scenario simulations
